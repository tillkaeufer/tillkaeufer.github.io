DuCKLinG is a fast 1D model that can be used to interpret JWST/MIRI spectra of protoplanetary disks.
For an introduction to DuCKLinG, see the [first DuCKLinG paper](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024A%26A...687A.209K/abstract) that benchmarked the model and fitted the GWLup.
Additionally, this model has been applied [in a paper](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024arXiv240806077K/abstract) to disentangle the dust and gas contributions of the JWST/MIRI spectrum of a very low-mass star.
If you are interested in using the model, [please contact me](/contact).
