My name is Till Käufer and I am Astronomy PostDoc at the [University of Exeter]( https://www.exeter.ac.uk/).   
I did my PhD as part of the [CHAMELEON network](https://chameleon.iwf.oeaw.ac.at/) at the [Centre for Exoplanet Science](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/exoplanets/) of the [University of St&nbsp;Andrews](https://www.st-andrews.ac.uk/).  
I did an one-year secondment at [SRON](https://www.sron.nl/) associated with the [Kapteyn Astronomical Institute](https://www.rug.nl/research/kapteyn/) in Groningen and I finished my PhD at the [IWF](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/en/iwf) in Graz, Austria.  
My research deals with protoplanetary disks. I develop techniques based on Machine Learning to link astronomical observations to theoretical models. This focused in recent years on the modelling of JWST/MIRI spectra. Currently, I am especially interested in the impact of dynamical disk processes on disk observations.
